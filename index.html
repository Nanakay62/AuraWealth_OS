Complete Aura User Guide (User Manual)
1. 🎛️ Dashboard (Your Money Home Screen)

Think of this as the main control panel for your money.

  • Total Net Worth: Your big total score. It adds up all the money in your bank, mobile wallets, cash, and investments into one big number.
  • Remaining Today: Your daily allowance. If your budget gives you GH₵50.00 for the day and you buy lunch for GH₵30.00, this number drops to GH₵20.00 left. If you spend less today, your limit for future days automatically goes up!
  • Daily Baseline: The standard target amount you aim to spend each day (like GH₵50.00/day).
  • Payday Countdown: A progress tracker counting down the days until your next payday or allowance arrives.
  • Quick Tip: Tap or hover over any day bar on the chart to see exactly how much you spent on that date.
  • Cash & Accounts Overview: A fast look at how much money is sitting in each of your main wallets right now.
2. 💳 Accounts / Vaults (Your Digital Pockets)

This is where your money actually lives day-to-day.

  • Supported Pockets: MTN Mobile Money, Telecel Cash, AT Money, Bank Accounts, Physical Cash at home, and USD Cash.
  • Balance Updates: You can manually fix or update the numbers in any wallet whenever you want.
  • Quick Transfer: Move money from one pocket to another (like moving money from your Bank to your MoMo wallet) without counting it as spent money.
  • Empty Pocket Alert: If you try to spend money from a wallet that has GH₵0.00, the app alerts you so you don't accidentally log money you don't have.
3. 📈 Portfolio (Your Savings & Investments)

This section keeps your long-term money separate from your daily spending money.

  • Investment Types: Track Treasury Bills (T-Bills), GCB Master Wealth, Stocks, or custom investments.
  • Growth Tracker: Shows when your investments mature and how much interest or profit they are making.
  • Empty Cards: If you have GH₵0.00 in an investment category, it fades into the background so your active investments stay front and center.
4. 💸 Transactions (Logging Money In & Out)

This is where you log everything you spend or earn.

  • Expense / Income Switch: Use the top toggle to switch between Expense (money going out) and Income (money coming in).
  • Expense Mode (Yellow Button): Tap category buttons (Food, Transport, Bills) or use One-Tap Presets (+ GH₵10 Transport, + GH₵25 Lunch) to log spending in two seconds. Pick which pocket (MoMo, Cash, Bank) you paid from.
  • Income Mode (Green Button): Pick an income type (Salary, Freelance, Gift) or use presets (+ GH₵2,000 Payday). The app fills in the details, lets you edit the exact amount if needed, and adds the money back to your chosen wallet.
  • History Tabs: Look back at your past activity split into Expenses, Income, and Transfers.
5. 🤝 Debts & Receivables (Who Owes Who)

Never forget who borrowed money from you or who you need to pay back.

  • Lent (Money Owed to You): Keep track of money you gave to friends or family. You can log partial repayments until they pay you back completely.
  • Borrowed (Money You Owe): Track loans or money you borrowed, complete with due dates so you pay them back on time.
  • Wallet Boost: When someone pays back a debt, that money goes right back into your cash wallets.
6. 📊 Reports & Summaries (Your Money Patterns)

See big-picture charts showing how you handle your money over time.

  • Yearly Overview: See your total spending for the year, your average monthly spend, and your highest spending month.
  • 12-Month Grid: A month-by-month chart (Jan-Dec) showing which times of the year you spend the most.
  • Category Breakdown: Pie charts showing what percentage of your money goes to Food vs. Transport vs. Bills.
7. ⚙️ Settings (App Controls & Sync)

Customize how your app works and looks.

  • Budget & Payday Setup: Change your baseline daily allowance, salary targets, and payday dates.
  • Cloud Sync Status: Shows if your data is safely backed up to the cloud (Cloud Synchronized) or saved offline on your device (Local Mode). It syncs automatically whenever you have internet.
  • Dark / Light Mode: Switch between Obsidian Dark Mode and Light Mode anytime.
