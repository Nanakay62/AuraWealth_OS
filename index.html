Complete Aura User Guide (Easy Manual)
🎯 Today's Daily Cash (Safe-to-Spend Engine)

Baseline Plan: Imagine you have GH₵1,500 for a 30-day month. Your starting goal is to spend no more than GH₵50.00 every day (GH₵1,500 divided by 30 days). That keeps your daily pocket money clean and equal!

Dynamic Compass: If you buy a big data bundle today or spend GH₵90, tomorrow's daily limit drops automatically so you do not run out of cash before payday. But if you spend GH₵0 today, your daily safe-to-spend limit goes right back up! It is like a smart compass guiding your wallet every morning.

📱 Mobile Money & Cash Wallets

Why Separate? Imagine having labeled piggy banks in your room: one for MTN MoMo, one for Telecel Cash, one for AT Money, one for your Bank Account, and one for Physical Cash in your drawer. Keeping them in separate boxes inside the app means your app balances always match your real phone and bank balances down to the exact pesewa! No more guessing where your money went.

🛡️ Emergency Survival Time (Runway)

Survival Clock: The app looks at all your total spendable cash and divides it by your monthly expenses. It tells you exactly how many months you could keep living comfortably if you stopped making money today. If your clock shows 6 Months, you have 6 full months of safety if anything unexpected happens!

⚡ Payday 1-Click Piggy Banks

Auto-Sorting Salary: When payday arrives, you do not need to manually calculate where every pesewa goes. Set up your payday allocation rules once, and in just one tap, your paycheck splits automatically into Treasury Bills for long-term growth, Bank Savings for safety reserves, and Mobile Money for your daily spending!

💸 Airtime, Data Bundles & E-Levy Fees

Counting Extra Fees: Small expenses add up quickly! When you buy GH₵20 airtime, a GH₵50 data bundle, or pay a GH₵1 transfer fee (like E-Levy) when sending money to friends and family, logging those small fees keeps your digital wallets perfectly accurate down to the pesewa.

📄 Proof-of-Funds PDF Generator

Official Printouts: Need to show proof of your financial standing for official reviews, personal records, or bank checkups? Clicking export generates a clean, bank-style 1-page summary of all your liquid cash assets, investments, and net worth ready for printing!
